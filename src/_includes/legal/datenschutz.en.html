Principles

We process personal data confidentially, only for the purposes stated in this policy and only for as long as necessary for these purposes or as required by statutory retention obligations. We do not pass on personal data to third parties without your consent unless we are legally obliged to do so. This website can be visited without registration.

Where the GDPR applies, we base processing on the following legal bases under Art. 6(1) GDPR: your consent (a), the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (b), legal obligations (c) and our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning and understandable website (f).

Please note that data transmission over the internet, for example when communicating by email, may have security gaps. Complete protection of data against access by third parties is not possible.

Visiting the website

When you visit this website, your browser transmits technically necessary data to our hosting provider’s server, such as your IP address, date and time, the page visited, browser type and operating system. This data is required to deliver the website and ensure its security, and is not combined with other data about you.

Fonts and images are delivered together with the website. No content from other third-party providers such as Google Fonts is embedded.

Web analytics with Matomo

To understand how our website is used and to improve it, we use the open-source software Matomo. We operate Matomo ourselves at stats.getrestful.ch. The data collected is not passed on to third parties and is not used for advertising.

Matomo records in particular the pages visited, the length of the visit, the referring page, links clicked, browser, operating system, screen resolution, language and a shortened IP address. To do this, Matomo sets cookies on your device that make it possible to recognise returning visits. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the website remains fully usable.

We only use the analyses in aggregated form. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our website or, where required, your consent.

Contact and appointment booking

If you contact us by email, we process your details to answer your enquiry and for any follow-up questions.

For appointments, we link to booking pages of third-party providers (Calendly and Google Calendar). These pages only open when you click the link. The processing of your data on these pages is governed by the privacy policy of the respective provider.
